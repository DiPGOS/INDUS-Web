How DiPGOS carries a project through.

Follow one sentence from the tender to the site.

  1. 1Read the tender

    Upload the package. DiPGOS extracts the synopsis, client, value, dates and bid bond.

    4.12.2 Concrete in the dam body shall be placed and spread in even layers, each compacted before the next is laid.

  2. 2Understand the requirements

    Requirements are extracted for six disciplines. Engineers approve them; nothing goes forward unchecked.

    A civil requirement: place and spread the dam-body concrete in even layers, each compacted before the next. Approved by the civil engineer.

  3. 3Build the proposal

    Requirements become deliverables, and deliverables become operations.

    The dam body is the deliverable. Its operation is Place & spread, one compacted layer at a time.

  4. 4Export the bid

    The budget sheet comes out in Excel and the proposal in Word, with your commercial buffers applied.

    In the budget sheet, Place & spread is priced layer by layer.

  5. 5Book real resources

    Once the work is won, planned resource types become real, booked people, machines and materials.

    Booked for Place & spread: a placing crew, two dozers, a vibratory roller and the concrete for each layer.

  6. 6Control construction

    Site teams report, and alarms flag deviations as they happen.

    Today’s site report: six layers placed and spread, each compacted before the next. Checked against the requirement.
