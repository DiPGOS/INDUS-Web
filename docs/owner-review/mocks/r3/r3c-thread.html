Four systems, one project, and nothing connecting them.

  • Tenders take weeks to read.Hundreds of documents, addenda arriving daily, and requirements buried in specs.
  • Bids and builds don’t talk.The estimate lives in one file, the schedule in another, the site reports in a third.
  • Overruns show up too late.By the time a deviation reaches the monthly report, the money is already spent.

How DiPGOS carries a project through.

1Read the tender

Upload the package. DiPGOS extracts the synopsis, client, value, dates and bid bond, and runs your bid / no-bid decision with the right approvals.

2Understand the requirements

Requirements are extracted for six disciplines and mapped to the project’s areas. Engineers approve them; nothing goes forward unchecked.

3Build the proposal

Requirements become deliverables, deliverables become operations, and operations get their crews, equipment and materials. Schedule and cost derive themselves.

4Export the bid

The budget sheet comes out in Excel and the proposal in Word, with the proposal manager’s commercial buffers applied.

5Book real resources

Once the work is won, planned resource types become real, booked people, machines and materials, with work fronts, critical path and baselines.

6Control construction

Every project, from the portfolio down to a single operation, on a live map. Site teams report; alarms flag deviations; changes and cost increases show as they happen.

Six disciplines. Seven levels of drill-down. Every AI answer sourced.
