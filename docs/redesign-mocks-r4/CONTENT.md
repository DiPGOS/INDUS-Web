- **Intro:** "A bid is priced in one set of spreadsheets, planned in another, built against a third and reported in a fourth. DiPGOS carries the same requirements, resources and operations all the way through, so the numbers you bid are the numbers you build against."

| # | Step | Line | Module |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Read the tender | Upload the package. DiPGOS extracts the synopsis, client, value, dates and bid bond, and runs bid / no-bid with the right approvals. | CPDS |

| 3 | Build the proposal | Requirements become deliverables, deliverables become operations, and operations get their crews, equipment and materials. Schedule and cost derive themselves. | CPDS |
| 4 | Export the bid | The budget sheet comes out in Excel and the proposal in Word, with commercial buffers applied. | CPDS |
| 5 | Book real resources | Planned resource types become real, booked people, machines and materials, with work fronts, critical path and baselines. | Atom Manager |
| 6 | Control construction | Every project, from portfolio to a single operation, on a live map. Site teams report; alarms flag deviations. | ACCS |
